# Nigerian-E-commerce-Data-Analysis-

# E-commerce Performance Intelligence & Revenue Optimization — Nigerian Case Study

## Executive Summary
This analysis provides insights into the Nigerian E-commerce market, showing trends, top-performing products, and regional revenue distribution. The project aims to deliver actionable recommendations to optimize operations, improve revenue performance, and guide strategic market expansion.

## Business Problem
E-commerce businesses often face challenges in identifying high-performing products, profitable regions, and customer purchasing trends. Understanding revenue distribution, order patterns, and regional preferences is critical for making data-driven decisions that maximize profitability and operational efficiency.

## Objective
- Analyze sales data to identify top-performing products, regions, and branches.  
- Assess revenue trends and order status impact on overall revenue.  
- Provide actionable insights for strategic decision-making and market expansion.

## Dataset
- **Source:** [Kaggle public dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/babajidedairo/nigerian-ecommerce-sales-dataset?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
- **Features:**  
  - `Order ID`, `Branch Location`, `Branch Name`, `Business Name`, `Is Deleted`  
  - `Item ID`, `Item Name`, `Item Price`, `Order Item Number`, `Item Status`  
  - `Packed Quantity`, `Quantity`, `Total Price`, `Order Date`, `Order Region`, `Order Local Area`

## Methodologies
- Data Cleaning & Processing:** Handled missing values, duplicates, and data inconsistencies.  
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Identified trends, patterns, and outliers using Python (Pandas, NumPy).  
- Visualization: Created charts and dashboards using Seaborn, Matplotlib, and Jupyter Notebook.  
- Revenue & Performance Analysis: Grouped data by product, branch, region, and order status to quantify revenue impact.

## Skills Utilized
- Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)  
- Jupyter Notebook  
- Data Cleaning & Transformation  
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
- Data Visualization & Dashboarding  

## Results & Recommendations
- Total Revenue: ₦1.78 billion generated during the analyzed period.  
- Revenue by Order Status: 'Delivered' orders dominate revenue (₦1.23B, 68.8%), while 'Cancelled' orders contribute significantly (₦493.7M, 27.7%).  
- Monthly Revenue Trend: Shows an upward trajectory, indicating business growth.  
- Top Products:** High-demand items include "Milo Sachet 20g," "Knorr Chicken," and "Maize."  
- Branch & Regional Performance: Lagos branch and region lead revenue (₦1.74B and ₦1.58B, respectively), highlighting Lagos as a key market.  
- Order Volume vs. Value: 'Delivered' orders have higher average order value (₦1.16M) compared to 'Cancelled' orders (₦332K).  
- Regional Product Preferences:** "Milo Sachet 20g" tops in Lagos and 'Unknown,' while Ogun favors "Milo 20g" and "Milo Sachet 20g."  

Key Takeaways: 
1. Reduce cancellations to maximize revenue.  
2. Leverage Lagos as a strategic market hub.  
3. Tailor product offerings to regional preferences to drive growth.
